Excavator Tilt Rotator

A compact, electrically actuated tilt rotator built for a 1/12-scale excavator, bringing more realistic attachments to teleoperation research and operator training.

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

A compact attachment for a scaled excavator

At MIT's The 77 Lab, I helped design and build an electrically actuated tilt rotator for a 1/12-scale excavator. It lets the bucket tilt and rotate, supporting research into teleoperation and operator training.
