ARES

ARES is a 30-pound combat robot competing in the National Havoc Robotics League (NHRL). As a primary member of the ARES team, I design, manufacture, and implement a variety of parts of the robot, as well as operate the weapon at several competitions a year.

Excavator Tilt Rotator

An excavator tilt rotator is an attachment that increases an excavator operator's degrees of freedom from three to five, allowing for greater flexibility and control. As a researcher with MIT's 77 Lab, I developed a fully functional 1/12-scale tilt rotator for teleoperation and operator training research.
